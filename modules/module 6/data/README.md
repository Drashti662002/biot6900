# BIOT 6900 · Module 6, Part 3 cohort

> ⚠️ **THE PHENOTYPE IS SIMULATED.** 1000 Genomes has no disease labels. The genotypes, alleles and score weights are real; the `phenotype` column in `meta.csv` was generated by a computer simulation (described below). AUC and calibration results are illustrative of a real phenomenon, not measurements of real disease risk.

Variant IDs are rsIDs and are identical across the three variant-bearing files. Dosages count the **ALT** allele as defined in the VCF.

## Sources

- **Score:** PGS Catalog **PGS000334** (GRSfull_22, Alzheimer's disease; Zhang Q et al., *Nat Commun* 2020, doi:10.1038/s41467-020-18534-1). 22 SNPs including **APOE** rs429358 (ε4) and rs7412 (ε2). Harmonized scoring file, **GRCh37** positions.
  - The original file publishes no `other_allele`. `other_allele` is the PGS Catalog's harmonization inference (`hm_inferOtherAllele`); where it listed several possibilities, the one matching the 1000 Genomes REF/ALT was used. Effect alleles and weights are unchanged.
- **Genotypes:** **1000 Genomes phase 3** (release 20130502, **GRCh37**), biallelic SNPs only. Matched to the score by chromosome, position and alleles (the VCF has no rsID for the two APOE SNPs).
- **Ancestry:** super-population from `integrated_call_samples_v3.20130502.ALL.panel`. AFR and AMR include admixed populations (e.g. ASW, ACB, MXL, PUR).

## How the phenotype was simulated

Random seed **6900**. Designed so that the portability drop comes from **real LD differences**, not from the score itself:

1. For each score (tag) variant, a hidden **causal** variant was chosen at random among 1000 Genomes SNPs within ±250 kb with **r² > 0.6 with the tag in EUR** and EUR minor-allele frequency ≥ 5%. Two tags with no such neighbour (rs11218343, rs9896864) are their own causal.
2. Each causal variant got a true per-allele effect equal to its tag's published weight, signed by the direction of its EUR LD with the tag.
3. Liability = genetic score from the causal variants (scaled to SD 1) + 0.35 × (age − 60)/8 + Normal(0, 2.09²) noise. Age ~ Normal(60, 8).
4. The top 30% of liability across the whole cohort are cases. **The same rule applies to every ancestry.**

The noise SD was tuned so the score's AUC in EUR is about 0.67. Mean r² between tags and their causal variants: EUR 0.82, AFR 0.44, EAS 0.73, SAS 0.73, AMR 0.75.

**APOE caveat.** Both APOE SNPs were given LD proxies like every other variant. In AFR the proxy for rs429358 (ε4) has r² ≈ 0.01 with ε4, so in this simulation **ε4 has almost no effect on disease in African-ancestry samples**. That is a consequence of the design, **not real biology**: ε4 is a risk allele in African-ancestry cohorts too, though with a weaker effect than in Europeans.

## Limitation

`genotypes.csv` contains **only these 22 score variants**. A student who swaps in a PGS Catalog score for a different disease will find almost none of its variants in `genotypes.csv`, and the notebook will compute a PRS from the few that overlap (often zero). Using other diseases' scores would need a much larger genotype file.
